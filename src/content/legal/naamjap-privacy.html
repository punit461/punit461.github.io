Last updated: 12 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Naam Jap Counter (Android package com.naamjap.counter), a mantra chanting counter made by Punit Bharadwaj (RKTechnolabs).

1. Information we collect

None. Naam Jap Counter does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal or device information.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in a database on your device only: your chanting counts, sessions and their duration; the mantras you add, hide or write yourself; your sankalps (vows); and your settings such as language, theme, sounds and reminder times. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If Android device backup is turned on in your Google account, Android may include this data in your own backup.

3. Backups you make

If you use Back Up to a File, the app creates a file with the data above and opens Android's share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice. Restore from Backup only reads a file you pick.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you turn on daily reminders or the streak alert. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback when you count. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Run at startup: so scheduled reminders continue after the phone restarts.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts.

5. Spoken mantra

The optional Mantra Voice feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services) to recite the selected mantra. The mantra text is passed to that engine on your device; please see your text-to-speech provider's own policy.

6. Sharing your progress

If you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens with a short text summary of your progress. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
