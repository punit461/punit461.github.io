Last updated: 11 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Tap Attack (Android package com.tapattackfocus.game), a reaction game made by Punit Bharadwaj (RKTechnolabs).

1. Information we collect

None. Tap Attack does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal or device information.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.

2. Information stored on your device

Your best scores, stars, XP and level, daily challenge streak, achievements and settings (sound, vibration, dot skin) are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: short haptic feedback when you tap. You can switch it off in the game's settings.

The app requests no other permissions.

4. Sharing your score

If you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens with a short text message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

5. Children

The game contains no inappropriate content and collects no information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
